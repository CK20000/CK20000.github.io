Motor Driver Evolution

Four FOC iterations + prototype stages

Overview

A progression of motor driver boards built to close the loop between power electronics and control firmware. Started with a hand-wired H-bridge prototype, then a fabricated half-bridge PCB, then into FOC driver iterations — each revision fixing a specific limitation from the previous one.

01 · Full-Bridge Prototype

Hand-wired H-bridge built on perfboard before I designed any PCBs. Gate drivers, MOSFETs, deadtime handling, and current sense wired point-to-point. Ugly, but it worked — the build that proved the topology well enough to commit to a fabricated design.

Full-bridge prototype — hand-wired, working.

02 · Isolated Half-Bridge PCB

First fabricated motor driver — a single isolated half-bridge. Established the design language for later boards: IR2101 gate drivers, 6N137 optocoupler isolation between logic and power, and AD8418 bidirectional current sensing across a 2 mΩ shunt.

Isolated half-bridge — first fabricated driver PCB, logic and power isolated.

03 · FOC Driver · v1

First three-phase FOC driver board. Established the topology: low-side shunt sensing, optocoupler-isolated gate signals, and a discrete power stage driven from an STM32G431. Exposed the design limits that motivated the next revision — thermal density and gate-loop layout.

04 · FOC Driver · v2 (current)

Current driver revision — cleaned up power routing, improved thermal behavior, and refined the gate-drive layout after v1's lessons. Runs the STM32G431 sensorless FOC kernel at 24 kHz.

  • Refined power stage layout — reduced gate loop inductance
  • Improved thermal path from MOSFETs to board copper
  • Integrated bulk capacitance and snubbers at the power entry
  • Same 24 kHz control loop, cleaner electrical behavior
FOC v2 — current board, revised layout with improved thermal and gate-drive behavior.

05 · FOC Driver · v3 (in progress)

Next revision in design. Focused on integrating the sensing front end more tightly with the power stage and reducing overall board size for a more compact final form factor.

STATUS · IN DESIGN

Layout and component selection in progress. Will be added here when fabricated and validated.
