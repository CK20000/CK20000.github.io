What it is

A ruggedized, student-accessible PLC platform built around the STM32F407VET6. Designed for educational lab environments — combines real industrial communication protocols (Modbus RTU over RS-485) with proper field isolation so students can safely learn industrial automation on hardware that behaves like the real thing.

Key features

  • Full field isolation — 8 opto-isolated digital inputs (PC817), 8 opto-isolated outputs, and 4 isolated PWM channels (6N137)
  • Analog interface — 8 buffered 0–10 V inputs (resistor dividers + Zener clamping) and 2 DAC outputs via LM358 op-amp stage
  • Industrial comms — half-duplex RS-485 (MAX3485) with selectable 120 Ω termination; Wi-Fi via ESP-12S on USART3
  • Expansion — I2C (OLED/peripherals), SPI2 (external IO modules), onboard 8 Mbit SPI flash for data logging
  • Debug + DFU — 4-pin SWD header and USB-C DFU interface
