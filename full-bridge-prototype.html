Full-Bridge Motor Driver

Prototype build · Pre-PCB era

What it is

A hand-wired full H-bridge motor driver built before I started designing PCBs. Gate drivers, MOSFETs, deadtime handling, and current sense wired point-to-point on perfboard. Ugly, but it worked — and it's the build that proved I understood the topology well enough to commit to a fabricated board.

Key features

  • Full H-bridge topology — four switching elements driving a single motor phase
  • Hand-wired on perfboard — no PCB, all point-to-point
  • Gate drive timing validated against real switching behavior
  • Predecessor to the isolated half-bridge PCB design
