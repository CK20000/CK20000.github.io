Li-ion Battery Packs

Hand-built · Three configurations

What they are

Three hand-assembled Li-ion battery packs built for FPV platforms. Each pack is spot-welded from individual cells — series nickel interconnects, parallel bus bars where applicable, heat-shrink sleeve, XT60 main output, and a JST balance charging lead.

The three packs

6S1P · Pack 1 22.2 V nominal · 25.2 V max · series-only configuration, higher voltage for larger props.
6S1P · Pack 2 Second 6S1P pack — matched pair for longer flight time without changing platform voltage.
4S3P · 10 Ah 14.8 V nominal · 16.8 V max · 24 A continuous discharge · built for the 10-inch platform.
