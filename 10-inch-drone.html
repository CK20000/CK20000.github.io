10-Inch FPV Drone

Custom build · Long-range platform
type:10-inch quadcopter use:FPV · long-range role:Full build & integration

What it is

A 10-inch quadcopter built from scratch as a testbed for flight-controller firmware work and long-range FPV. Assembly covered frame build, power distribution, ESC wiring, motor mounting, radio and video link integration, and full flight tuning.

Key features

  • 10-inch propeller class — larger platform for stable flight and payload margin
  • Full assembly and integration — frame, motors, ESCs, flight controller, receiver, and video system
  • Used as the flight-test platform for the custom H743 Betaflight port
  • Radio link setup and flight controller tuning for stable manual flight
