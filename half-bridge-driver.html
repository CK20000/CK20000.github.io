Galvanically isolated half-bridge — logic and power domains separated by 6N137 optocouplers.

Isolated Half-Bridge Motor Driver

2025 · KiCad · Galvanic PWM isolation + bidirectional current sensing
gate driver:IR2101 mosfets:2× IRFZ44N isolation:2× 6N137 sensing:AD8418 + R002 ref:1.65 V buffered

What it is

A single half-bridge motor driver designed to safely interface a microcontroller with high-voltage motor rails. The logic side (MCU signals) and power side (motor rail) are fully isolated through high-speed optocouplers, eliminating ground bounce and protecting the MCU from high di/dt switching events.

Key features

  • Bootstrap high-side drive — 1N5819 diode + 1 µF cap, requires periodic low-side pulses (no 100% duty cycle)
  • Galvanic isolation — 2× 6N137 high-speed optocouplers between logic and power
  • Precision current sensing — AD8418 bidirectional amplifier across a 2 mΩ shunt, heavily filtered (10 Ω / 10 nF / 1 nF)
  • Center-biased reference — buffered 1.65 V rail via MCP6002 for symmetric bidirectional measurement
  • 1000 µF bulk smoothing — handles switching transients on the power rail
